SocGholish and StealC

Run a WordPress site? If your site was on the published apex list, it was serving a fake browser update to your visitors. Check it, then remove the injected script and rotate all admin credentials - see the guide below.

SocGholish is a malware downloader. It gets onto legitimate websites, very often WordPress sites, and shows visitors a fake browser update. People who run that "update" get an information stealer, which takes saved passwords and other credentials from their computer.

Two different groups are affected, and they need different things: the owners of the compromised websites, and the people who ran the fake update.

02Background

Operation Endgame is a coordinated international law enforcement operation against criminal malware infrastructure, run with the Netherlands, France, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Canada, Belgium and Australia, alongside Europol and Eurojust.

This site covers two episodes of season 3 that produced victim data: S03E03 SocGholish, made public by the Dutch National Police on 18 June 2026, and S03E04 StealC and Amadey, made public on 24 June 2026, which involves over 26 million email addresses, too many for individual notification, which is why individuals are directed to Have I Been Pwned instead.
