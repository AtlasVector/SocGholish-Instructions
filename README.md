# socgholish-instructions

Multilingual SocGholish and StealC instructions for WordPress admins and
victims, in English, Dutch, French and Arabic, plus a client-side checker over
DIVD's published apex lists.

**Independent. Not affiliated with DIVD, the Dutch National Police, Europol or
any vendor.** This repository links to official sources and does not speak for
them or on their behalf.

## Background

Operation Endgame is a coordinated international law enforcement operation
against criminal malware infrastructure, involving the Netherlands, France, the
United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Canada, Belgium and Australia, with
Europol and Eurojust.

This repository covers the two episodes of season 3 that produced victim data:

- **S03E03, SocGholish**, made public by the Dutch National Police on
  18 June 2026. Covers the SocGholish downloader, the compromised WordPress
  sites that serve it, and the stealer it delivers.
- **S03E04, StealC and Amadey**, made public on 24 June 2026. Covers credentials
  stolen by the StealC infostealer and information identifying infected
  machines. It involves over 26 million email addresses, which is why
  individuals are directed to Have I Been Pwned rather than notified one by one.
